A few things to know.

Will it work with my speakers?

Designed for stereo USB output devices with no input channels, on macOS 14.2+ and Apple Silicon. Hardware compatibility varies. Apps must output to the selected speaker to appear as controllable.

How do the three volume levels work together?

Master, app, and tab volume combine. An app at 100% follows the master level; reducing it adds attenuation. Tab controls live in the extension, not the Mac panel. Safari has no per-tab support; app grouping depends on the audio processes reported by macOS.

Why does macOS ask to record audio?

The permission lets the app read playback audio, adjust its level, and send it to your speakers. Audio is processed in memory and is never saved or uploaded. Media keys separately require Accessibility permission.

What happens when I quit?

The speaker returns to its original volume, which may be louder. Pause playback before quitting or stopping control. Disconnecting leaves the app on standby; reconnecting asks whether to enable it.

What are the current limitations?

The system volume slider may remain disabled. Creating an app audio route can briefly interrupt playback. Tab settings reset after a browser restart. Protected media and other audio processors may affect compatibility.
